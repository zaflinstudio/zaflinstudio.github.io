Privacy & Support Contact: contact.zaflin@gmail.com

2. Information Processed by the Game

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ZAFLIN stores certain gameplay data exclusively in local device storage using standard Android sandbox storage (via SharedPreferences and local application document storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any developer server. It includes:

  • Active Game Progress & Snapshots: The 9×9 board tile arrangement, current score, 3 piece-tray slots, combo streak, and active power-up statuses, stored so you can resume your game seamlessly if the application is closed or restarted.
  • High Score: Your best score record.
  • User Settings & Preferences: Sound effects toggle, background music / ambient audio toggle, and haptic vibration toggle.
  • Daily Elemental Unlock Progress: The number of completed rewarded advertisements (0, 1, or 2) watched for each of the 12 Elemental worlds for the active local calendar day, and the local date key (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • Run Statistics: Completed run count used locally to govern the frequency capping of interstitial advertisements.
  • Tutorial State: Whether you have completed or dismissed the introductory gameplay guide.

B. Information Processed by Third-Party Advertising Technology

ZAFLIN integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, version 5.3.1) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements and collect consent preferences. When your device is connected to the Internet, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process certain device and network data in accordance with Google's independent privacy policies. This data may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, or other device/network identifiers.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location inferred from your IP address. (ZAFLIN does not request, collect, or access precise GPS location data).
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad views, clicks, video completion events, and technical diagnostic telemetry needed to deliver ads, verify rewards, and prevent fraudulent ad traffic.

For more information on how Google processes user data in advertising, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

3. Advertisements and Rewarded Actions

ZAFLIN uses advertising to support its free-to-play model:

  • Rewarded Video Advertisements: These ads are strictly optional and only play when you explicitly request an in-game benefit:
    • Daily Elemental Themes: Watching two completed rewarded ads grants temporary access to any of the 12 Elemental worlds for the active local calendar day. (Classic mode is always free and requires zero ads).
    • Change Pieces Power-Up: Watching a completed rewarded ad refreshes all 3 tray pieces (limited to once per game run).
    • Elemental Blast Power-Up: Watching a completed rewarded ad charges a power-up to clear an occupied cell's row and column cross (limited to once per game run).
  • Interstitial Advertisements: Full-screen ads that may appear conditionally at natural game-over transitions when selecting Play Again. Interstitials are strictly frequency-gated:
    • Zero interstitials are shown during your first three completed runs.
    • Thereafter, an interstitial may be considered at most once every three completed runs (runs % 3 == 0).
    • A mandatory minimum 90-second cooldown is enforced between interstitial impressions.

Ad availability depends on network connectivity and Google AdMob inventory. In Classic mode, the game can be played entirely offline without an Internet connection.

Where required by regional data protection regulations (such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK GDPR, and applicable US state privacy laws), ZAFLIN employs Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present a consent-gathering interface on launch.

You may review or modify your advertising consent preferences at any time within the game:

If you are located in a region where UMP is not required by law, Google Mobile Ads serves advertisements in accordance with applicable platform policies and your device-level advertising settings.

5. Purposes of Processing

Information is processed exclusively for the following verified purposes:

  • Core Gameplay Delivery: Maintaining board state, piece generation, and score calculation.
  • Local Persistence: Restoring active game runs and remembering sound/haptic preferences.
  • Advertising Delivery & Verification: Delivering rewarded and interstitial ads, verifying completion to grant power-ups, and preventing fraudulent impressions.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Collecting, applying, and maintaining consent records via Google UMP.
  • Support Communication: Responding to user inquiries or privacy requests submitted to contact.zaflin@gmail.com.

6. Data Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data collected by third-party SDKs is shared directly with Google LLC and its advertising partners for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention purposes as disclosed above.

Because Google operates global infrastructure, information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transferred to and processed on servers located in the United States and other countries outside your country of residence, under Google's data transfer mechanisms and standard contractual clauses.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Device Data: Retained on your device indefinitely until you clear application data via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > ZAFLIN > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data) or uninstall the application.
  • Support Inquiries: Emails sent to our support address are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request and any directly related follow-up, after which they are deleted.
  • Third-Party Ad Data: Retained by Google in accordance with Google's published data retention policies. We do not maintain or possess independent copies of this data.
