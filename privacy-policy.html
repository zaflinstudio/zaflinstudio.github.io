This Privacy Policy describes how ZAFLIN — Elemental Block Puzzle (“ZAFLIN”, “the Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) treats information when you install, access, or play the application on Android devices.

2. Information Processed by the Game

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ZAFLIN stores certain gameplay data exclusively in local device storage using standard Android sandbox storage (via SharedPreferences and local application document storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any developer server:

  • Active Game Progress & Snapshots: 9×9 board cell occupation, current score, 3 piece-tray slots, combo streaks, and active power-up states, stored to seamlessly restore your game when closing and reopening the app.
  • High Score: Your best score record.
  • User Settings & Preferences: Sound effects on/off, background music/ambient audio on/off, and haptic vibration on/off.
  • Daily Elemental Unlock Progress: The number of completed rewarded advertisements (0, 1, or 2) watched for each of the 12 Elemental worlds for the active local calendar day, and the local date key (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • Run Statistics: Completed run count used locally to govern the frequency capping of interstitial advertisements.
  • Tutorial State: Whether you have completed or dismissed the introductory gameplay guide.

B. Information Processed by Third-Party Advertising Technology

ZAFLIN integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, version 5.3.1) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements and collect consent preferences. When your device is connected to the Internet, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process certain device and network data in accordance with Google's independent privacy policies:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, or other device/network identifiers.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location inferred from your IP address. (ZAFLIN does not request, collect, or access precise GPS location data).
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad views, clicks, video completion events, and technical diagnostic telemetry needed to deliver ads, verify rewards, and prevent fraudulent ad traffic.

For more information on how Google processes user data in advertising:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Advertisements and Rewarded Actions

ZAFLIN uses advertising to support its free-to-play model:

  • Rewarded Video Advertisements (Optional):
    • Daily Elemental Themes: Watching two completed rewarded ads grants temporary access to any of the 12 Elemental worlds for the active local calendar day. (Classic mode is always free and requires zero ads).
    • Change Pieces Power-Up: Watching a completed rewarded ad refreshes all 3 tray pieces (limited to once per game run).
    • Elemental Blast Power-Up: Watching a completed rewarded ad charges a power-up to clear an occupied cell's row and column cross (limited to once per game run).
  • Interstitial Advertisements: Conditional full-screen ads shown at natural game-over transitions when selecting Play Again:
    • Zero interstitials are shown during your first three completed runs.
    • Thereafter, at most once every three completed runs (runs % 3 == 0).
    • Minimum 90-second cooldown between interstitial impressions.

In Classic mode, the game can be played entirely offline without an Internet connection.

5. Purposes of Processing

Information is processed exclusively for:

  1. Core gameplay delivery (board state, piece generation, score calculation).
  2. Local persistence (restoring active runs and remembering audio/haptic settings).
  3. Advertising delivery & verification (serving ads, verifying reward completion, fraud prevention).
  4. Regulatory compliance (collecting and maintaining consent records via Google UMP).
  5. Support communication (responding to user inquiries submitted to contact.zaflin@gmail.com).

6. Data Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data collected by third-party SDKs is shared directly with Google LLC and its advertising partners for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention purposes. Information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transferred to and processed on servers located in the United States and other countries under Google's data transfer mechanisms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Device Data: Retained indefinitely until you clear application data via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > ZAFLIN > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstall the application.
  • Support Inquiries: Retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request, after which they are deleted.
  • Third-Party Ad Data: Retained by Google in accordance with Google's published data retention policies.
